## Limitations

- DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, Kimi K3.1, Muse Spark Open, Mistral Large 4, Clover,
  Ripple, Limite, Flint, MiniCPM-OCR 1.0, Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe,
  Nemotron-Embed and LFM3 have no local GGUF: their profiles follow the
  upstream PRs and model cards, their floors are unit-tested, the plans are
  not measured.
- The HIP two-GPU split remains slower than Vulkan for some hybrids (v5.6.3
  finding); the Bolt + LiLiCorr HIP figure shows the same pattern.
- VRAM figures from `--list-devices` in the new-model script were not
  reliable on this driver and are not reported.
- Throughput figures are single-machine measurements; representative runtime
  tests are not a universal model/context/quality guarantee.
